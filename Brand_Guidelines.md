# APEX QUANTS LLP — Brand Design & Color Palette Guidelines

> **Purpose:** This document defines the visual identity system for Apex Quants LLP. Use this as the single source of truth when generating any branded content, UI, presentations, documents, or marketing assets.

---

- **Company Name:** APEX QUANTS LLP
- **Tagline (Primary):** Engineering Scalable Digital Systems & Quantitative Solutions

## 2. COLOR PALETTE

### Primary Colors

| Role              | Name            | HEX       | RGB               | Usage                                      |
|-------------------|-----------------|-----------|-------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| Background        | Jet Black       | `#000000` | rgb(0, 0, 0)      | Page/slide/canvas backgrounds              |
| Brand Accent      | Neon Lime Green | `#A8FF00` | rgb(168, 255, 0)  | Headings, highlights, CTAs, logo fill, dividers |
| Primary Text      | Pure White      | `#FFFFFF` | rgb(255, 255, 255)| Body text, paragraph copy, contact info    |

### Secondary Colors (Inferred / Complementary)

| Role              | Name            | HEX       | Usage                                      |
|-------------------|-----------------|-----------|--------------------------------------------|
| Muted Text        | Light Gray      | `#CCCCCC` | Captions, subtitles, secondary labels      |
| Dark Accent       | Charcoal        | `#1A1A1A` | Card backgrounds, subtle section dividers  |
| Hover/Active      | Bright Yellow-Green | `#C8FF40` | Interactive states, hover effects         |

### Color Rules
- **Never** use the neon green as a background for large sections — it is an **accent only**.
- **Never** place white text on the neon green — use black text (`#000000`) on green surfaces.
- The **black + neon green** pairing is the brand's signature contrast. Always maintain this.
- Avoid introducing reds, blues, or purples — they conflict with the brand palette.

## 3. TYPOGRAPHY

### Font Strategy
The PDF uses a bold, geometric sans-serif family throughout. The closest system-safe and widely available equivalents are listed below.

| Element           | Recommended Font          | Weight      | Style     |
|-------------------|---------------------------|-------------|-----------|
| Brand Name / Logo | Montserrat / Futura       | 900 (Black) | Uppercase |
| Section Headings  | Montserrat / Futura       | 700 (Bold)  | Title Case |
| Taglines          | Montserrat / Inter        | 700 (Bold)  | Sentence case |
| Body Copy         | Inter / Open Sans         | 400 (Regular) | Sentence case |
| Labels / Accents  | Montserrat / Inter        | 600 (SemiBold) | Uppercase or Title |

### Typography Rules
- The brand name "APEX QUANTS LLP" must **always appear in ALL CAPS**.
- Headings use the **neon lime green** (`#A8FF00`) color on dark backgrounds.
- Body text uses **white** (`#FFFFFF`) on dark backgrounds.
- Avoid serif fonts entirely — they conflict with the brand's technical identity.
- Minimum body font size: **14px** (digital), **10pt** (print).
- Letter spacing on headings: slightly expanded (`0.05em` to `0.1em`).

## 4. LOGO GUIDELINES

### Logo Description
- A geometric, abstract letterform resembling a stylized **"A"** shape.
- Constructed from two curved, asymmetric leaf/boomerang shapes forming an arch.
- The logo uses **neon lime green** fills on a **black background**.
- The mark is bold, minimal, and modern — suggesting precision and upward movement.

### Logo Variants

| Variant           | Background  | Logo Color  | Usage                              |
|-------------------|-------------|-------------|------------------------------------|
| Primary           | Black       | Neon Green  | Default — all official materials   |
| Reversed          | White       | Black       | Light background contexts only     |
| Monochrome        | Black       | White       | B&W print, embossing               |
| Icon Only         | Black       | Neon Green  | App icons, favicons, small formats |

### Logo Rules
- **Minimum clear space:** Equal to the height of the "A" mark on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** 32px height (digital), 0.5 inch (print).
- Do **not** recolor the logo in any color other than the approved variants.
- Do **not** add drop shadows, gradients, or outlines to the logo.
- Do **not** stretch or distort the logo's proportions.
- The wordmark "APEX QUANTS LLP" may appear below or beside the icon, never above.

## 5. LAYOUT & DESIGN SYSTEM

### Core Design Principles
- **Dark-first:** All primary layouts use a black (#000000) base.
- **High contrast:** Neon green on black is the dominant visual pattern.
- **Minimal decoration:** The brand avoids gratuitous gradients, textures, or complex illustrations.
- **Bold typography:** Large, heavy headings dominate the visual hierarchy.

### Dividers & Decorative Elements
- The brand uses a **horizontal line divider with a centered snowflake/asterisk icon** (✦ or ❖) as a section separator.
- Lines are **white or neon green** on black backgrounds.
- Dividers are thin (1–2px) and elegant, not thick bars.

### Spacing
- Generous vertical whitespace between sections.
- Content is center-aligned for hero/cover pages.
- Body content is left-aligned.
- Padding within content blocks: minimum 32px (digital) / 0.5 inch (print).

### Layout Grid
- Single-column layout for documents and brochures.
- Centered logo and headline on cover pages.
- Text-heavy sections use left-aligned single column with ample line height (1.6–1.8x).

---

## 6. ICONOGRAPHY & VISUAL STYLE

- Icons should be **line-style or minimal solid**, in **white** or **neon green**.
- No stock illustration or complex imagery — the brand is code/data/system-oriented.
- Any decorative symbol should be geometric and precise (e.g., ✦, ◆, →, ⬡).
- Charts or diagrams should use the brand palette: black background, green data elements, white labels.

## 7. VOICE & MESSAGING FRAMEWORK

### Brand Voice
- **Authoritative:** Speaks as a technical expert, not a generalist.
- **Precise:** Uses specific terminology (TWAP, VWAP, VaR, LLM, ETL, CVaR, etc.).
- **Elevated:** Avoids casual language; uses "engineering," "architecting," "crafting" over "building" or "making."
- **Confident:** Action-driven statements, not hedged or passive.

### Key Message Pillars
1. **Scalability** — Systems built to grow.
2. **Security** — Enterprise-grade, compliance-first.
3. **Intelligence** — AI-driven, automation-first.
4. **Expertise** — Deep quant and fintech specialization.

### Headline Formula
- Verb + Noun + Value: *"Engineering Scalable Digital Systems"*
- Three-part rhythm: *"Crafting Code. Engineering Excellence. Elevating Businesses."*

---

## 8. DO's AND DON'Ts SUMMARY

### ✅ DO
- Use black backgrounds as the default canvas.
- Use neon lime green (`#A8FF00`) for all key highlights and headings.
- Use bold, heavy sans-serif fonts in all caps for brand name.
- Maintain high contrast at all times.
- Use precise, technical language in copy.
- Keep designs clean, minimal, and structured.

### ❌ DON'T
- Don't use warm colors (orange, red, yellow) — they break brand cohesion.
- Don't use light/white backgrounds as the primary canvas.
- Don't use decorative or script fonts.
- Don't add gradients to the logo.
- Don't use casual, vague, or non-technical language.
- Don't clutter layouts — whitespace is intentional.
